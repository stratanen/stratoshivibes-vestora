# Vestora: κείμενα για το Solana dApp Store

## App name
Vestora

## Short description (EN, max 30 words)
Track crypto, stocks, ETFs and real estate in one place, compare them with simple alternatives, and plan ahead with honest ranges. Your data stays on your device.

## Long description (EN)
Vestora shows how your investments are really doing and helps you plan what comes next.

Your alternative story: see what the same amounts, on the same dates, would be worth today in the S&P 500, the Nasdaq 100, Bitcoin or a 2% deposit.

Measured properly: return of your choices (TWR) next to the return of your money (MWR), so you can see what timing cost or gained you.

Your plan: set a goal, a monthly amount and a time horizon. Vestora runs 400 simulated market paths and shows a range, not a single promise: how often you reach your goal, what it would take, and how a second plan compares.

Built for real life: financial resilience checks (emergency fund, unstable income), the drop you said you could tolerate, market stress tests, dividends, interest and staking rewards, and a decision journal.

Everything in one place: crypto, US and European stocks and ETFs, Athens stocks, gold, deposits, real estate or anything else with your own price. Connect your Seeker wallet to import SOL, tokens and xStocks (read-only).

Private by design: no account, no tracking, your data stays on your device, with encrypted backups that only you can open. Vestora never asks for signatures, seed phrases or private keys.

Pro unlocks while your wallet holds SKR worth 20 € or a Seeker Genesis Token. SKR is never spent or locked.

Available in English and Greek (chosen from your phone's language, switchable in Settings).

Vestora describes and explains. It does not give investment advice and never tells you what to buy or sell.
